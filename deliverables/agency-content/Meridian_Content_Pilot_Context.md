# Meridian Field Services — Agency Content Pilot Context

**Project type:** Fictional, self-directed portfolio simulation  
**Canonical portfolio owner:** `meridian-field-services` GitHub repository  
**Agency workstream:** Hossain Consulting controlled content and conversion system  
**Decision owner:** Hemayet Hossain, CEO  
**Status:** Approved foundation; evidence build not yet started  
**Approved:** 19 September 2026

## Disclosure

Meridian Field Services is a fictional training and portfolio scenario. It is not an employer, client or operating HVAC contractor. Customers, jobs, approvals, records and metrics used in this pilot must be synthetic. No public content may imply that Meridian accepts real bookings or has delivered commercial results.

## Pilot definition

- **Business:** Meridian Field Services
- **Trade:** Residential HVAC
- **Pilot service:** Residential wall-mounted split-system preventive servicing
- **Service area:** Alexandria, Sydney, NSW
- **Ideal customer:** Owner-occupiers in Alexandria with an existing wall-mounted split-system air conditioner who want preventive servicing, documented findings and approval control over additional repairs.

The audience definition is a simulated target segment, not a demographic or housing-market claim about Alexandria.

## Approved service scope

- Booking and equipment details captured in Salesforce
- Technician inspection checklist
- Safe cleaning within the approved simulated service scope
- Basic operational and performance checks
- Photo-supported findings
- Written service report
- Separate quote requiring customer approval before additional repairs
- Automated follow-up and maintenance reminder

The portfolio must not describe regulated HVAC work procedures or imply that Hemayet personally performed licensed trade work. Technical fields and checklists are workflow demonstrations reviewed against an appropriate fictional-business approval model.

## Approved differentiation

> Every HVAC service includes a documented inspection checklist, photo-supported findings and a written quote for approval before any additional repair work begins.

This differentiation will be demonstrated through Salesforce records and workflow evidence rather than unsupported marketing claims.

## Approved objections

1. “It’s still cooling—why does it need servicing?”
2. “Why can’t you quote any repair before inspecting it?”
3. “Another provider offers a cheaper service.”

Content must answer these questions without inventing failure probabilities, energy savings, equipment-life improvements, repair prices or competitor comparisons.

## Approved proof package

- Salesforce booking and customer record
- Synthetic equipment and service history
- Technician inspection checklist
- Sanitised before-and-after demonstration images
- Photo-supported findings report
- Customer approval record for additional work
- Completed service report
- Automated follow-up and maintenance reminder
- Test results showing the workflow operated correctly

The proof package must not contain invented reviews, customer counts, revenue, savings or claims of commercial deployment. Demonstration images must be owned, appropriately licensed or deliberately created for the simulation; they must not be presented as real Meridian customer jobs.

## Approved brand voice

> Calm, practical and transparent. Plain Australian English, short explanations, no scare tactics, no technical showing-off and no pressure. Explain what was checked, what was found, what can wait and what requires action.

Approved style example:

> Your system may still be cooling while airflow or drainage is beginning to deteriorate. We inspect it first, document what we find and obtain your approval before quoting additional repair work.

The example remains a draft until technical review confirms that its implied statements are appropriate for the specific content and evidence.

### Prohibited tone and claims

- “Your air conditioner could fail at any moment.”
- “Act now before it costs thousands.”
- Unsupported health, indoor-air-quality, energy-saving or equipment-life claims
- Fake urgency, scarcity, deadlines or guaranteed results
- Claims that Meridian is licensed, insured, established or operational unless clearly presented as fictional scenario data

## Approved pricing policy

No public price is approved at this stage. The pilot uses:

> Pricing depends on the number, type and condition of the systems. Meridian confirms the servicing scope and indicative price before the booking is finalised. Additional repair work is quoted separately and proceeds only after approval.

Any later simulated price model must document labour assumptions, travel, scope, GST treatment, exclusions, additional-unit pricing and the fact that the figures are synthetic. It must not be represented as a current Alexandria market price.

## Fields still blocked

- Licence/registration numbers
- Insurance claims
- Years operating and team size
- Public phone, email and booking destination
- Service price or price range
- Warranty terms
- Customer reviews/testimonials
- Measured commercial outcomes
- Real-job photography

These fields remain `NOT APPLICABLE — FICTIONAL SIMULATION` or `NOT YET DEFINED` unless a clearly labelled synthetic design decision is approved.
